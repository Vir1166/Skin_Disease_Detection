Skin Disease Detection

Skin diseases vary from harmless to serious. Early detection is key! Use our free skin detector to identify and manage your condition.

Skin Conditions

Acne

Common skin condition causing pimples, blackheads, and whiteheads from clogged hair follicles and oil glands.

Actinic Keratosis & Basal Cell Carcinoma

Rough, scaly sun-damaged skin patches that can progress to basal cell carcinoma if left untreated.

Bacterial Impetigo

Highly contagious bacterial infection producing red sores and honey-coloured crusts, most common in children.

Eczema

Chronic condition causing dry, itchy, and inflamed skin patches due to an overactive immune response.

Healthy / Normal Skin

Smooth, evenly toned, and well-hydrated skin with no visible lesions, rashes, or abnormal growths.

Melanoma, Nevi & Moles

Serious skin cancer developing from pigment-producing cells that can spread rapidly if not caught early.

Psoriasis

Autoimmune condition causing rapid skin cell build-up, resulting in scaling, red patches, and inflammation.

Vitiligo

Disorder where the skin loses pigment cells, leading to irregular white patches across the body.

Warts, Molluscum & Viral Infections

Common viral skin infections causing raised bumps that spread through direct contact.
